  • Effective ventilation is critical in drying chambers to remove evaporated solvents and moisture quickly. Proper airflow helps prevent defects such as bubbling or uneven finishes a key reason Leo Surface Coatings integrates industrial coating equipment with reliable exhaust and ventilation systems across our paint booth and oven exhaust incinerators.

    • Electric heating elements

    • Hot water or steam systems

  • High-quality filtration systems are essential to prevent contaminants from entering the chamber.
